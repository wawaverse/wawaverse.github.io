Greetings! Welcome to my blog. 🌸

I'm **Dani**, someone who loves reading, writing and working on creative projects in general. 🇮🇹

## A few things about myself

I live in Italy and spend most of my days going on walks, chatting with others and reading. Music 
is one of the distractions I'm fondest of, some of the genres I mostly listen to are Future Funk, 
Electronic, VOCALOID in general, Indie Rock & Jazz. I am able to speak 4 languages, some in better ways than the others, as follows (not in order):

- Italian
- English
- French
- Spanish

I use any pronouns and am non binary, but I usually default to gender neutral phrasing if I can, depending on the language I'm using at the moment.

## About the blog
This site is built with an emphasis on minimalism and high readability, using tools & components
from various talented people. Thank you for your work! 💜

- **Engine**: [Astro](https://astro.build)
- **Color Palette**: [Rosé Pine](https://rosepinetheme.com)
- **Typography**: [Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Variable](https://fontsource.org/fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible-next)
- **Emoji Library**: [Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji)
- **Syntax Highlighting**: [Shiki](https://shiki.style)
- **Icons**: Mostly from [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com)

All posts shared on this site are under the Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0[^first] license. You can find poetry ([under the dedicated poetry section](/poetry)), comments on the world around us and general thoughts and anecdotes from my life. The poetry section might look pretty empty right now, as I'm not sure whether to share parts of my work regarding that just yet. Check back in the future for new updates! :)
